Careers — Explore Our Job Opportunities

Join TULA, where we don't just build structures; we shape futures. As a leading real estate builders and developers company, we are committed to creating exceptional living spaces and fostering a culture of excellence and innovation. We offer dynamic career opportunities that empower individuals to thrive in a collaborative and supportive environment. If you are passionate about shaping the skyline of tomorrow and making a meaningful impact in the real estate industry, we invite you to explore career opportunities with us. Join our team and be part of a journey that transforms dreams into reality.

Sales Executive ⌄
Drive sales of residential and commercial properties. Build strong client relationships, identify new business opportunities, and meet monthly targets. Excellent communication and negotiation skills required.
Project Manager ⌄
Oversee end-to-end project delivery from planning to handover. Coordinate with architects, contractors, and vendors. Ensure timelines, budgets, and quality standards are met consistently.
Lead Architect ⌄
Lead design and planning of residential and commercial developments. Collaborate with engineering and interior teams to create functional, aesthetic, and sustainable spaces that reflect TULA's vision.
Brand Manager ⌄
Develop and execute brand strategy across all channels. Manage campaigns, oversee content creation, and maintain brand consistency. Work closely with sales and leadership to strengthen market presence.
